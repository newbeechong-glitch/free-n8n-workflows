## The free workflows

1. **Instant Lead Responder** (`01_instant-lead-responder.json`) — a new form lead gets scored 0–100, saved to Google Sheets, HOT leads ping your team on Slack within seconds, and every lead gets a welcome email from your Gmail. About 15 minutes to set up.

2. **Review Request Automator** (`02_review-request-automator.json`) — checks your customer sheet every morning and emails a polite Google-review request to customers whose job finished 3–30 days ago. Marks them asked, so nobody ever gets asked twice.

3. **Content Repurposer** (`03_rss-to-ai-social-drafts.json`) — watches your blog's RSS feed, drafts a LinkedIn post, an X post and a short-video hook for each new article, and saves them to a Google Sheets review queue. Nothing is ever auto-posted.

4. **Gmail Attachment Saver** (`04_gmail-attachment-saver.json`) — checks your Gmail every hour, saves every attachment to Google Drive with a tidy date_sender_filename name, and logs each file to a Google Sheet so you can find it later. A message-ID check means the same file never gets saved twice. About 15 minutes to set up.
